# Collision Audit and Environment Blockout Audit

Generated by `scripts/qa_collision_audit.gd`. Raw data, one row per obstacle: `qa_reports/collision_audit.json`.
Run it with `godot --headless --path . -s res://scripts/qa_collision_audit.gd [-- --levels=0,1]`.

## Method
- The tool loads every campaign level (Prologue, Chapters 1-10, Finale). It lists every visible `MeshInstance3D` and root `CSGShape3D` whose largest world dimension is at least 0.45 m.
- It classifies each one with the same rules as the runtime pass and records whether physics covers it. Coverage can be authored (a parent body or `use_collision`) or auto-generated.
- It then validates the coverage:
  - Upright obstacles: a player-sized capsule (r 0.4, h 1.74, the same as the Player) is swept with `cast_motion` along X and Z through the obstacle centre. The obstacle passes if the sweep stops early.
  - Floors, bridges and ledges: a vertical ray drop must hit.
- Obstacles more than 2.2 m above the floor are marked overhead and excluded from failures.

## Fix: a reusable runtime pass
`systems/environment/EnvironmentCollision.gd` is attached automatically by `CampaignEnvironmentArt` (Chapters 1-10), `CavernArt` (Prologue) and `Finale.gd`. Two frames after the level's `_ready`, it gives every uncovered visible mesh a `StaticBody3D` on layer 1.

**Priority order for choosing the collision type:**
1. Metadata or group override: meta `collision` = none / convex / trimesh / trunk / box / lod3, or group `no_auto_collision`. This is checked on the mesh and its ancestors.
2. Per-asset table for the optimized Tripo set (`ASSET_RULES`, following TRIPO_DELIVERY_REPORT.md):

   | Assets | Collision |
   |---|---|
   | wall, floor, glass barrier, crate, radio | box |
   | pillar, tent, boulder, stalagmite, turbine | convex |
   | bridge span, arch portal | trimesh built from the `_LOD3.glb` |
   | vaelor key | none |

3. Name keywords, closest node first:

   | Keywords | Collision |
   |---|---|
   | fern / grass / plant / leaf | none |
   | tree | cylinder trunk (lower 60%, about 12% of width) |
   | wall / floor / ruin / cave | trimesh |
   | rock / boulder / prop | convex |

   Anything else gets convex.

Root CSG shapes that have no collision get `use_collision = true`. The Prologue's procedural cave builds its own collision: a trimesh for the shell and the ground, a convex hull for each rockfall rock, and a cylinder for each stalagmite.

**Level instances now point to the optimized models.** They use `assets/models/optimized/*_LOD0.glb` with Godot's automatic mesh LOD. The bounds of LOD0 match the originals to within 0.5%, which I checked for every model, so the existing transforms are unchanged.

## Environment blockout audit (all levels)
I reviewed screenshots from `scripts/qa_environment_capture.gd` (windowed, 1280x720): `screenshots/environment/final_L*_*.png`, with an overview in `final_contact_sheet.png`.

**Reusable fix applied to every level:** `systems/environment/BlockoutDresser.gd`. It replaces missing materials and flat untextured materials on CSG and procedural meshes with triplanar materials:
- `materials/cave_ground.gdshader` on flat pieces
- `materials/cave_rock.gdshader` on upright pieces

The audit now finds 0 remaining white or flat surfaces. Before, there were 12:
- Ch2 floor, gate and counterweight
- Ch3 ledge
- Ch4 floor
- Ch7 monument and mural
- Ch9 terrain and falling rock
- Ch10 floor and pillar core (both had no material)
- Finale ground (no material)

### P0: still reads as blockout
- **Chapters 2, 3, 4, 7, 9 and 10:** the player stands on an endless flat plane in fog with no enclosing walls or ceiling and no silhouettes. They need cave shells. `systems/environment/CaveBuilder.gd` is profile-driven (`profile_z/w/h`) and can be reused for each of them.
- **Finale:** a 100 m textured plane under a black sky. The sediment tiling repeats visibly, and there is no ridge, sky or daylight even though the level is meant to be a daylight escape.

### P1

- **Chapter 10:** the floor colour is graded purple and flat, and the central pillar is a plain cylinder.

### P2

- **Chapter 2:** the gate and counterweight are CSG boxes.

### P3

## Prologue (benchmark): what changed
- **Cave geology** (`systems/environment/CaveBuilder.gd`), built procedurally as an irregular shell rather than a cylinder:
  - Bedded limestone with stepped ceiling slabs and bedding-plane partings.
  - Vertical fractures and flared wall bases.
  - Ledges in the tall spaces.
  - Changing width and height along the route: a squeeze at z≈-4, an 11 m vertical chamber at z -10 to -15, a second squeeze, then the portal hall.
  - About 70 stalactites in clusters, kept clear of the route.
  - 22 stalagmites with collision.
  - About 900 pebbles (300 on the Low preset).
  - A rockfall that seals the route behind the start, with fallen limestone slabs.
  - End caps at both ends, so there is no black void.
- **Ground:** a heightfield using `cave_ground.gdshader`. It blends sediment, gravel, exposed rock, mud and glossy wet puddles through height-blended vertex masks, and has talus slopes against the walls. The old box floor is kept only as a safety collider below the ground.
- **Lighting:**
  - Filmic tonemap with exposure 1.05; the ambient level is dark but not black.
  - Distance and height fog. Glow is enabled but limited to the High preset by GraphicsSettings.
  - Volumetric fog is enabled for Forward+ and High only. The project uses the Compatibility renderer, so it currently has no effect.
  - On Balanced and High, mist cards are used instead of volumetric fog. On Low they are hidden through `set_graphics_quality`.
  - Three warm expedition lamps; the dropped one at the start flickers.
  - Cyan Vaelor glow at the relic and the portal, with emissive veins in the rock that strengthen toward the portal.
  - A cold shaft light down the vertical chamber.
- **Environmental storytelling:**
  - A crushed crate in the rockfall.
  - Mara's thrown radio, which ties into her "Radio... Nadiya?" line.
  - The descent rope hanging down the shaft, with its coil on the floor.
  - Orange survey flags along the old route.
  - A Vaelor carved panel in the chamber wall.
  - The glass barrier, which looked like two white planes in the screenshots, is now hidden.
- **Water placeholders:** `CaveBuilder/WaterPlaceholders` holds Marker3D nodes in group `water_placeholder` with meta `kind=puddle`. The water system under `systems/water/` can use them. To let it draw its own puddle surface, set the ground shader's `puddle_enabled` to 0. There is no foliage in the Prologue.
