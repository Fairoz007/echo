# ART BIBLE: ECHOES BENEATH

## 1. Visual Pillars
- **Grounded Realism with Ancient Mystery:** Believable, weather-worn environments where archaeological structures feel millennia old, covered with natural decay, water erosion, and moss.
- **Atmospheric Chiaroscuro:** Deep contrast between sunlight filtering through dense jungle canopies and eerie darkness underground pierced only by Mara's flashlight and ancient bioluminescent veins.
- **Architectural Signature (The Vaelor):** Heavy cyclopean stonework carved from dark resonant basalt and obsidianite. Geometric concentric soundwave carvings rather than typical hieroglyphs.

---

## 2. Color Palette & Lighting Hierarchy
- **Biome 1: Mist Valley & Surface Ruins**
  - Dominant colors: Deep moss green (#2B472A), damp earth brown (#3D2F22), misty slate grey (#6C7A89), warm golden morning sun highlights (#F4D06F).
- **Biome 2: Subterranean Fissures & Deep Caves**
  - Dominant colors: Wet cavern limestone (#222426), mineral ochre (#8C6D46), cold subterranean blue mist (#1F2A38), crisp white LED flashlight beam (#EDF2F7).
- **Biome 3: Drowned Temple & Lost City**
  - Dominant colors: Aquamarine subterranean water (#1A4B5C), ancient verdigris bronze (#437057), resonant acoustic obsidianite (#111317), glowing cyan acoustic nodes (#00E5FF).

---

## 3. Protagonist Design (Mara Vale)
- **Silhouette:** Athletic, functional expedition gear.
- **Clothing:** High-durability canvas utility cargo pants, weather-resistant technical expedition jacket with rolled sleeves, climbing harness with carabiners and chalk bag.
- **Equipment:** Leather backpack with rolled bedroll, shoulder-mounted tactical LED light, field climbing pick slung at the hip, radio transceiver on shoulder strap.
- **Material Response:** PBR roughness mapping with dynamic rain/water wetness darkening and roughness diminution when submerged.
