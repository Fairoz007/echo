# GAME DESIGN: ECHOES BENEATH

**Genre:** Third-person cinematic action-adventure / exploration / mystery  
**Engine:** Godot 4.7.2 (DirectX 12 Forward+, Jolt Physics 3D)  
**Target Frame Rate:** 60 FPS locked  
**Perspective:** Third-Person Over-the-Shoulder & Cinematic Follow  
**Protagonist:** Mara Vale (Expedition field archaeologist & survivalist)  

---

## 1. Core Pillars
1. **Physicality & Tactile Exploration:** Traversal feels grounded. Mara climbs ledges, balances on logs, squeezes through fissures, and wades through water with momentum.
2. **Environmental Archaeological Mystery:** The ruins of the Vaelor and the Heart Vault communicate history through architectural resonance, water diversion, and forgotten stonecraft.
3. **Pacing of Contrast & Discovery:** Seamless transitions between expansive vistas (Jungle, Lost City) and claustrophobic subterranean caves.
4. **Calculated Survival Combat & Stealth:** Dangerous, lethal encounters where flanking, stealth takedowns, and environmental traps take precedence over mindless shooting.

---

## 2. Character Mechanics & Controls
- **Locomotion:** Walk (1.8 m/s), Run (4.5 m/s), Sprint (7.0 m/s), Crouch (1.4 m/s).
- **Traversal:** Ledge grab, shimmy, mantle, vault over low obstacles, jump with physics inertia, crawl through tunnels.
- **Tools & Equipment:** Flashlight (toggleable battery/focus), Field Climbing Pick, Survival Knife, Expedition Bow / Semi-auto Sidearm, Resonant Vaelor Tuning Fork.
- **Vitals:** Dynamic Health (regenerates partially up to 30%, medical kits for full recovery), Stamina bar for climbing sprints/swimming breath, Vibration/Audio awareness indicator.

---

## 3. Puzzle Design Framework
- All puzzles are grounded in physical world systems:
  - **Hydraulic:** Diverting water wheels, sluice gates, and buoyancy platforms.
  - **Gravitational:** Counterweight pulleys, stone balances, falling log anchors.
  - **Acoustic/Resonance:** Tuning forks and acoustic stones responding to frequencies to unlatch ancient Vaelor locking tumblers.
- Zero arbitrary minigames; all puzzle interactions occur directly in the 3D game world.

---

## 4. Audio & Presentation Philosophy
- Binaural reverb reflecting chamber volumes (narrow wet cave vs colossal subterranean cathedral).
- Dynamic musical score adapting between exploration mystery, tactical stealth tension, and high-adrenaline setpieces.
- Minimalist HUD that fades during quiet exploration and displays contextual climbing/interaction cues only when relevant.
