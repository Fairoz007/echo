# STORY BIBLE: ECHOES BENEATH

## 1. Lore & The Vaelor
Centuries before modern history, the **Vaelor** inhabited the deep sub-basins beneath the southern mist ridges. Unlike civilizations that mined stone for raw architecture, the Vaelor carved chambers into an ultra-dense, acoustic mineral stratum dubbed **Resonant Obsidianite**. They discovered this mineral resonated harmonically with geothermal tremors and subterranean acoustic vibrations, creating natural acoustic networks and kinetic locks powered solely by sound harmonics and water pressure.

At the core of their kingdom lay **The Heart Vault**—an immense spherical geode chamber containing a massive planetary seismic node. When early Vaelor priests attempted to harness its frequency to drive kinetic city-wide mechanisms, catastrophic frequency feedback caused tectonic shearing, sealing the underground kingdom in an eternal silence.

## 2. Characters & Factions

### Mara Vale (Protagonist)
- **Role:** Field Archaeologist & Lead Surveyor.
- **Traits:** Astute, physically resilient, pragmatic, morally driven.
- **Background:** Raised on mountain expeditions; values preserving history over monetizing relics.

### Dr. Elias Vance (Expedition Leader & Antagonist)
- **Role:** Head of the archaeological venture funded by the Obsidian Syndicate.
- **Traits:** Brilliant, obsessed with legacy, covertly calculating.
- **Arc:** Starts as Mara's mentor; revealed in Chapter 8 to have sold out the team to plunder the Vault's harmonic core for commercial acoustic weaponry.

### Mateo Cruz (Field Engineer & Logistics)
- **Role:** Expedition communications technician and mechanic.
- **Traits:** Grounded, loyal, cautious. Provides radio dialogue and camp support.

### The Obsidian Syndicate (Enemy Mercenaries)
- Heavily armed private security force contracted by Vance. Deployed across ruins and cave checkpoints with tactical assault rifles, flashlights, and search flares.

## 3. Campaign Narrative Progression
- **Prologue: The Descent:** Flash-forward; Mara wakes injured in collapsed subterranean ruins, discovers a resonant glyph, hears the low frequency hum.
- **Chapter 1: The Arrival:** 72 hours earlier; base camp in lush mist valley, team dynamics, discovering the First Sealed Portal.
- **Chapter 2: The First Ruin:** Hydraulic counterweight puzzle, discovering the "Voice Below" tablets.
- **Chapter 3: The Collapse:** Bridge breach sequence; ground gives way, team split; timeline connects to Prologue.
- **Chapter 4: Alone:** Subterranean cave navigation, radio silence, finding 19th-century abandoned expedition relics.
- **Chapter 5: The Intruders:** First contact with Syndicate mercenaries; stealth and tactical takedowns.
- **Chapter 6: The Drowned Temple:** Water-level manipulation, sunken aqueduct navigation, diving through flooded chambers.
- **Chapter 7: The Lost City:** Expansive underground reveal; sprawling cyclopean architecture, waterfalls, optional tombs.
- **Chapter 8: Betrayal:** Reconnecting with Elias; discovery of his contract with Syndicate; theft of the Harmonic Key.
- **Chapter 9: The Heart Road:** Tremors intensify; cavernous escape and pursuit through breaking infrastructure.
- **Final Chapter: The Heart Vault:** Multi-phase confrontation with Elias and Syndicate heavy unit in the resonant acoustic nexus.
- **Epilogue: Daylight:** Escaping the collapsing mountain ridge into the morning sun; journal closure and survivor reunion.
