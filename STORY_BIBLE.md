# STORY BIBLE: ECHOES BENEATH

Canon revision 2. This bible matches the voiced screenplay (COMPLETE_SCREENPLAY.md) and the recorded dialogue. Earlier drafts that cast Elias as the antagonist, used "Mateo Cruz" or named the "Obsidian Syndicate" are retired.

## 1. Lore & The Vaelor
Centuries ago the **Vaelor** lived in the deep basins beneath the southern mist ridges. They built in **resonant stone**, a dense mineral that carries sound the way roads carry people. Their gates, sluices and counterweights ran on water pressure and harmonics.

At the core of the kingdom lies **the Heart Vault**, a spherical geode that amplifies the mountain's seismic hum through four outer harmonic **nodes**. The Vaelor kept using the Vault after they knew it was unstable ("it worked, until it didn't"). Feedback sheared the mountain, and they sealed it, leaving warnings in the ruins: *the stone listens*, and *the Voice Below*.

## 2. Characters (voice IDs are pinned; see AUDIO_REPORT.md)
- **Mara Vale**, field archaeologist and protagonist. Pragmatic, resilient, believes history must be protected, not extracted. She narrates the chapter recaps as field-journal entries.
- **Dr. Elias Voss**, expedition leader and Mara's mentor. Brilliant and trusting to a fault. Injured in Chapter 8; he survives.
- **Nadiya Rahman**, communications and logistics, mostly heard over the radio. She is the team's steady voice.
- **Tomas Reyes**, climber and rescue lead. Pulls Mara out of the mountain in the finale.
- **Adrian Kell**, the survey specialist and **the betrayer**. He sold the expedition's coordinates to Meridian and let the Acoustic (Vaelor) Key fall to Drake. His regret, and the service road he reveals, does not erase what he chose.
- **Lucian Drake**, Meridian commander and antagonist. He means to weaponize the Vault's resonance network.
- **Meridian**, a private military contractor. Its armed teams patrol the caves, aqueduct and Heart Road.

## 3. Campaign Progression
- **Prologue: The Descent.** Flash-forward. Mara wakes beneath the mountain, finds a humming glyph and cuts to "72 hours earlier."
- **C01: The Arrival.** Camp, team introductions, the survey anomaly and the sealed Vaelor door on the eastern ridge.
- **C02: Whispers in Stone.** A hydraulic counterweight ruin and the inscription about "the Voice Below."
- **C03: The Fall.** A bridge crossing; the anchors were cut. Mara falls alone into the fissure, closing the loop to the Prologue.
- **C04: Beneath.** Cave survival, radio repair and an old survey camp. Nadiya warns that Meridian has arrived.
- **C05: Hunters.** Stealth past Meridian patrols. Drake orders the aqueduct secured, and supply crates prove someone leaked the route.
- **C06: The Drowned Temple.** Draining the flooded sanctuary through water-level puzzles; the lower gate rises.
- **C07: City of Echoes.** The underground city, the catastrophe mural and the optional Tomb of Weights.
- **C08: The Betrayal.** Elias is injured and Adrian is exposed. Drake has the Key, and Adrian offers the service road.
- **C09: The Heart Road.** Meridian blasting collapses the conduits during a pursuit to the Vault threshold.
- **C10: The Heart Vault.** Mara disables four nodes. Feedback breaks Drake's control, and she tells him to run.
- **Finale: The Mountain Breaks.** Escape to daylight; Tomas pulls Mara out, and everyone survives.
- **Epilogue: Daylight.** The team chooses preservation. The site's existence is proven and its location is kept secret.

## 4. Tone Rules for Dialogue
Grounded, cinematic and brief. Lines react to what the player sees; nobody narrates the controls. Radio lines are band-limited. Mara stays calm under pressure and only shouts in real danger.
