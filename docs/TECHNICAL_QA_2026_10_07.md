## Graphics profile and renderer findings

Low forces a 960 × 540 window and 30 FPS cap, disables antialiasing/shadows/expensive screen effects, reduces foliage and LOD. Balanced/High expose higher settings. Profile save/reload, authored High restoration and `--safe-graphics` override pass; tests restore the exact original user settings file. Water tessellation measured 162/1,250/8,450 triangles in Low/Balanced/High with unchanged height. Menu regression verifies three connected quality choices and the explained Low fullscreen restriction. Evidence: `docs/graphics_profiles_latest.log`, `docs/menu_graphics_latest.log`. These are settings/geometry tests, not different physical GPU tests.

D3D12 attempts failed with resource/vertex-array errors and 0x887a0005 device removal; one crashed with signal 11. Both concurrent and isolated failures were observed. Retained evidence: `docs/campaign_physical_d3d12_device_loss_failure.log`, `docs/campaign_physical_isolated_d3d12_failure.log`, `docs/campaign_physical_device_removed_failure.log`. Compatibility became the safe default. D3D12 remains an optional backend and must not be described as stable based on a short boot.

An earlier D3D12 finite stationary FPS sample on RTX 2060 measured Chapter 1 lowest average 92.5/minimum 89 FPS, other averages 211.4–240.1, with 2 s warmup/3 s sample per chapters 0–11 while editor/route ran. It cannot establish renderer stability, worst-case traversal, sustained performance or a target-hardware budget. Raw log: `docs/rendered_performance_latest.log`. Current Compatibility reduced settings are a test on this same RTX 2060, not certification of weaker GPUs.
