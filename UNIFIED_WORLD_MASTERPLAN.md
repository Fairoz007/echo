# Unified World Masterplan: Vaelor Valley and the World Beneath

Status 2026-10-11: **prototype stage.** One seamless handoff (Ch5 → Ch6) is implemented and tested. Every other chapter boundary still goes through `GameManager.load_chapter` (story card + scene swap).

## 1. Baseline (what exists)
- **Level layout:** 13 separate level scenes. Each is authored around its own origin, and Mara always travels **−Z** from a spawn near +Z.
- **Per-level nodes:** every level contains its own `Player`, `CameraController` (top-level follow camera) and `HUD`. Level scripts bind them via `@onready $Player` / `$HUD`.
- **What `_ready` builds:** levels assemble most of their geometry procedurally in `_ready`:
  - `systems/levelb/*Dressing.gd` (Ch1, 5, 6, 8, Epilogue)
  - `EnvironmentCollision.attach`
  - `BlockoutDresser`
  - `CampaignExploration` (dial tombs)
- **Saves** store `current_scene.scene_file_path`, the chapter, the player transform in that scene's coordinates, and per-level fields (`SaveManager.gd`).

## 2. Architecture chosen: chained regions with origin rebasing
A single giant scene was rejected. Instead, each existing level becomes a **region** that stays at its authored origin.

**Handoff sequence** (`systems/world/RegionHandoff.gd`):
1. **`prepare(path)`** runs while Mara approaches the boundary. It threaded-loads the next region scene plus every asset its `_ready` loads synchronously (`content/region_manifests.json`, generated by `scripts/build_region_manifests.py`).
2. **`hand_off(from, chapter, path, entry)`** runs at the threshold:
   - It translates the whole current region, including the top-level camera, so Mara's position coincides with the next region's entry point. Everything moves together, so nothing visibly jumps.
   - It moves the persistent `Player`/`CameraController`/`HUD` into the new region in place of the region's own copies, before the region's `_ready` runs, so `@onready $Player` binds to the same Mara.
   - It sets the chapter, objectives and checkpoint, makes the new region `current_scene`, and frees the old one.
3. Because the new region stays at its authored origin, **existing saves, checkpoints and level scripts keep working unchanged.**

**Why rebasing rather than placing all regions in one coordinate frame:**
- It avoids float precision drift across kilometres.
- It needs no re-authoring of 13 levels' coordinates.
- It keeps save compatibility.

The trade-off: only the current region and the next region coexist in memory, so long sightlines into distant regions need impostors (see §4).

## 3. Verified (Ch5 → Ch6)
Covered by `dev/region_handoff_test.gd` (Xvfb, LOW, real `move_forward` input into the trigger). PASS on:
- Same Player instance carried over.
- Health 63 and ammo 7 preserved.
- Camera yaw preserved.
- Camera pivot moves at most 0.028 m in any frame.
- Chapter is 6, and the Ch6 objective is active.
- Mara is standing on the Ch6 landing floor.
- The Ch5 region is freed, and exactly one player exists in the tree.

## 4. Known gaps (not yet seamless)
1. **The scenery swaps instantly** at the Ch5 gate: Ch5's ruin gate is replaced by Ch6's flooded hall. There is no physical passage yet. Next step: an authored connector tunnel or aqueduct bend at both ends, so the swap happens out of sight.
2. **Main-thread hitch at the threshold.**
   - In this container (software GL), the swap frame took **4.1 s** with prefetch, down from 5.4 s without.
   - The headless CPU profile of Ch6 `_ready` puts **≈3.0 s in `Chapter6Dressing.build`**, 0.55 s in CampaignExploration and 0.27 s in BlockoutDresser.
   - Real Windows hardware will be faster, but this hasn't been measured.
   - Fix path: time-slice the dressing build across frames during `prepare`, building into a detached node and then attaching.
3. **The other 11 boundaries still use the story card and scene swap.**
4. **Not yet tested:** save/load immediately after the handoff (the old region's per-level state is discarded by design, because Ch5 is one-way), and weather and audio continuity (Ch6 calls its own `WindSystem.set_preset` and Soundscape).
5. **The prologue flash-forward** still needs its own controlled entry.
